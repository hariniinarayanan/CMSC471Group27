Weather Patterns Across the U.S., 2017

Each bubble is a weather station, averaged over one month. Move the slider to see how the pattern between latitude and temperature changes through the year.

Y-axis: average temperature (°F). X-axis: station latitude. Bubble size: average daily precipitation. Color: region. Hover a bubble for details.

Station map, colored by average temperature for the selected month. Scroll to zoom and drag to pan. Hover a dot for details.

About this visualization

Dataset

Daily U.S. weather measurements for 2017 from NOAA's Global Historical Climatology Network (Daily), with sparse stations filtered out. Each row is one station on one day. I average rows into station-months. Temperature is in °F and precipitation is in inches per day. Where TAVG is missing, I use the mean of TMIN and TMAX. The question: how does the relationship between latitude and temperature change with the seasons, and where does rain fall?

Interactions

  • Dynamic filter (Category I): a month slider that filters the data to one month at a time.
  • Pan and zoom (Category II): scroll to zoom and drag to pan the station map, to inspect crowded regions like the Northeast.
  • Details on demand (Category II): tooltips on both charts showing station name, state, region, and values.
