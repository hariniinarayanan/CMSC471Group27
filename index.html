Weather Patterns Across the U.S., 2017

Each bubble is a weather station. Move the slider to see how the pattern between average temperature and precipitation or wind speeds changes throughout the year.

Selected date: January 1, 2017

Y-axis: average temperature (°F). X-axis: average daily precipitation or average daily wind speeds. Bubble size: daily range of temperature. Color: region. Hover over a bubble for more details.

Station map, colored by average temperature for the selected month. Scroll to zoom and drag to pan. Hover a dot for details.

Interactions

  • Dynamic filter (Category I): a date slider that filters the data to one date at a time.

  • Details on demand (Category II): tooltips on both charts showing station name, state, region, and values.
